Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect on overtonelab.com, what we use it for and how you can exercise your rights. It applies to people who book a call, contact us on WhatsApp or by email, or visit this site.

1. Who is responsible

Overtone Lab, a social media content agency based in Medellín, Colombia, represented by Andrés Giraldo. Contact: contacto@overtonelab.com · WhatsApp +57 302 445 1526.

2. What data we collect

  • What you enter in the booking form: full name, links to your social media, a description of your business, your 90-day goal, monthly investment range, email, WhatsApp number, who referred you and how soon you want to start.
  • The day and time of the call you choose.
  • If you contact us by WhatsApp or email, the details and messages you send us.

3. What we use it for

  • To schedule and hold the discovery call, and to send you the invitation and reminders.
  • To learn about your business before the call and assess whether our service fits you.
  • To contact you about this service by email or WhatsApp.

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Use of Google Calendar, Google Meet, Sheets and Gmail

To schedule calls we use the Google Calendar API with Overtone Lab's own account. This connection is used only to check the availability of our own calendar and to create your call event, with a Google Meet link and an invitation sent to your email. We store the booking form answers in an Overtone Lab Google spreadsheet, and we send from Overtone Lab's Gmail account only the reminder emails for your call. We do not access the calendar, email or Google data of the people who book. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Who we share it with

Only with the providers needed to run the service: Google (Calendar, Meet, Sheets and Gmail) for scheduling, video calls, the request log and reminders, and the hosting and messaging providers we use to operate the site. They may be located outside Colombia. This site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google servers.

6. How long we keep it

We keep it as long as needed to handle your request and the business relationship, and for as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

7. Your rights

Under Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 and its regulations, you have the right to know, update and correct your data, request proof of authorization, be informed about how we use it, file complaints with the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio, and revoke your authorization or request deletion of your data. To exercise them, write to contacto@overtonelab.com.

8. Security

We apply reasonable measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss or alteration. No system is completely infallible, but we work to reduce the risks.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
